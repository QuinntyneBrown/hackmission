Video player

The 20-second intro: a poster tile that opens a dialog player with captions and a transcript.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <video>

Overview

The video tile shows the mark on an ink poster with a gold play button; it opens the video dialog, which holds a native player with a captions track (off by default) and a transcript. Nothing autoplays with sound.

Anatomy

  1. PosterRadial ink gradient, mark at 5rem.
  2. PlayGold pill, 44px, labelled link.
  3. Meta (optional)Duration badge.

Responsive behaviour

  • < 640px the play button moves to the bottom-right corner of the tile so the mark stays visible.
  • The dialog's player keeps 16:9; the transcript scrolls beneath.

Theming

Poster and states use --color-bg-hero-deep and --color-border-hero; the play button is gold with ink text in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The tile's play control is a link to the dialog, labelled in full. The player is a native <video controls> with a <track kind="captions"> and a text transcript in a <details>; aria-describedby points the player at the transcript.

Keyboard

KeyAction
EnterOpens the dialog / plays.
Space, KPlay/pause in the native player.
CToggles captions (browser dependent).
EscCloses the dialog.

Focus

Opening the dialog focuses the close button; the player's controls follow; closing returns focus to “Watch the intro”.

Labelling

“Play the 20-second HackMission intro video”; the dialog title “HackMission in 20 seconds”.

Contrast

Play label on gold
Play button edge on poster
State text
State body

Motion

No autoplay; no looping; captions available but off by default; music under narration is mixed at −11 dB so speech stays intelligible.

Touch

Play button 44px; native controls are the browser's.

Content

  • Play label: "Watch the intro"; never "Click to play".
  • Transcript is the full narration, one paragraph per line.
