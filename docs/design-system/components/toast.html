Toast

A short message about something that just happened; errors stay until dismissed.

Overview

Toasts confirm what just happened ("You're on the list"), inform ("Confirmation email resent") or report a failure ("Couldn't add you", with Try again). They never carry the only copy of important information: the signup result is also shown in place.

Anatomy

  1. Title + body
  2. Action (optional)Retry / Undo / View.
  3. Dismiss

Variants

Confirmation email resent

Sent to amara.okafor@example.ca. Check spam if it isn’t there in 2 minutes.

Registration closes in 48 hours

Toronto · 6–7 Feb.

Success, info, warning (polite).
Couldn’t add you

Our mailing service didn’t respond. Your email is still in the form.

Danger (assertive) with an action; stays until dismissed.

Responsive behaviour

  • ≥ 640px: bottom-right, 24rem wide.
  • < 640px: full width at the bottom with env(safe-area-inset-bottom).

Theming

Raised surface with a 4px left border in the status colour; the icon follows the status.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The region is role="status" aria-live="polite" and exists in the DOM even when empty; error toasts use role="alert". Toasts are not focused when they appear.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the action and dismiss buttons in order.
EscDismisses the focused toast.

Focus

Focus is never moved to a toast; it stays where the person was.

Labelling

Title is the message; the action button says what it does.

Contrast

Title
Body
Success edge
Danger edge

Motion

Slides up over --duration-base; auto-dismiss after 8 s, paused on hover and focus; errors never auto-dismiss. Under reduced motion toasts appear and vanish without sliding.

Touch

Buttons 32px with 8px gaps; the toast is ≥ 56px tall.

Content

  • Title: what happened, past tense or state ("You're on the list", "Couldn't add you").
  • Body: what to do next, one sentence.
  • Action verbs: "Try again", "Undo", "View".
