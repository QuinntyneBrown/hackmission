Statement & pair

One giant line and one button beside an angled ink visual; and the two quiet statements side by side.

Overview

Two marketing blocks set the page's rhythm. The pair puts two short claims side by side. The statement is one giant headline with a gold word, a primary button and an angled ink panel holding the mark.

Anatomy

  1. HeadlineMax 11ch, one gold word.
  2. ButtonPrimary, large.
  3. VisualAngled clip-path, ink gradient, mark.

Variants

Built for churches and charities.

A real problem Friday. A working tool Sunday.

Open to everyone.

No experience required.

Pair.

Build something good for someone else.

Get notified
Statement.

Responsive behaviour

  • Statement: visual below the text < 1024px (4:3, clipped diagonal); absolute right panel ≥ 1024px.
  • Pair: one column < 768px.

Theming

Headline emphasis uses --color-brand-highlight; the visual uses the hero-deep tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Sections labelled by their headings; the visual is aria-hidden.

Content

  • Statement: one sentence, one gold word: "Build something good for someone else."
  • Pair: a claim of ≤ 5 words and a line of ≤ 10.
