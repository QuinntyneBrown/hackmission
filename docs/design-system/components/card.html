Card

A surface that groups one thing: an event, a result message, a CTA band.

Overview

Cards group related content on a raised surface. On the marketing site the signup result and the CTA band are card-like surfaces; in the organiser tools event cards list the season. A card is never the control: a clickable card has one real link.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards stack in one column below 768px; .grid--auto fills columns of ≥ 18rem above.
  • Padding stays --space-6; the CTA band keeps --space-10 vertical.

Theming

Surface background, default border, --shadow-1; dark theme raises the surface a step so the card still reads as lifted.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<article> (or <section>) with a heading. Interactive cards contain one link or button; the whole card is made clickable with a pseudo-element on that link, never with a click handler on the card.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the card's link/button.

Focus

Ring on the card's link; .card--interactive shows a ring on the card when its link is focused (:focus-within).

Labelling

Heading names the card; the badge adds the status in words.

Contrast

Body text
Muted text
CTA band text
Result on ink

Motion

Hover lift is a 1px translate over --duration-fast.

Touch

Interactive cards are ≥ 44px tall; the link inside is the target.

Content

  • Title is a noun phrase; the body one or two sentences.
  • One action per card, in the footer.
