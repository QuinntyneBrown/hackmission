Schedule

Four weekends, four cities: date overline, city headline, ink rule above each.

Overview

The schedule lists the season: an uppercase gold date, the city in extrabold, a 3px ink rule above each. When no season is announced the section becomes an empty state.

Anatomy

  1. Rule3px, --color-fg-default.
  2. DateUppercase, gold, tracked.
  3. CityExtrabold, 24–30px; qualifier muted.

Variants

  1. 6–7 Feb
    Toronto
  2. 17–18 Apr
    Vancouver
  3. 12–13 Jun
    Montréal
  4. 25–26 Sep
    Halifax + online
Four items.
