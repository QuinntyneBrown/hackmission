Empty state

When there is nothing to show yet: say why, and offer the one thing to do.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableIcon + title + action

Overview

An empty state explains why a space is empty and offers one action. On the home page, when no season is announced, the schedule section becomes an empty state pointing at the signup.

Anatomy

  1. Icon
  2. Title + one sentence
  3. One primary action

Variants

The 2027 season is being planned

We’re confirming host cities and partner organisations. Join the list and you’ll hear the dates before anyone else.

Get notified
First-run (season not announced).

No events match

Try another city or clear the filters.

No results (compact).

We couldn’t load the season

Check your connection and try again.

Error as empty.

Sizes

Full (icon, 48px padding) and compact (no icon, 24px padding) inside tables and cards.

States

The 2027 season is being planned

We’re confirming host cities and partner organisations. Join the list and you’ll hear the dates before anyone else.

Get notified
Empty states have no states of their own; the action carries them.

Responsive behaviour

  • Text wraps to 36rem; the action goes full width below 640px.

Theming

Muted text; the error variant tints the icon tile with danger tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A heading plus text; the action is a real link or button. No role needed; error empties may be role="status".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the action.

Focus

After a failed load focus stays where it was; the Try again button is next in order.

Labelling

The title explains the emptiness in words; the icon is hidden.

Contrast

Title
Body

Motion

None.

Touch

Action ≥ 44px on phones.

Content

  • Title: what is true ("The 2027 season is being planned"), not "Nothing here".
  • Body: why, and what will change it.
  • One action, the same verb as elsewhere ("Get notified").
