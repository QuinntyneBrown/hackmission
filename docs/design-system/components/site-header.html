Site header

Logo on the left, primary links, and the one call to action on the right. On the hero it sits on ink.

Overview

The site header is the top bar of every public page: the logo lockup, three section links (hidden below 768px) and the Get notified button. On the home page it sits inside the ink hero and uses the gold button; on inner pages it sits on paper and uses the ink primary.

Anatomy

  1. LogoLinks home.
  2. Primary nav<nav aria-label="Primary">, current link underlined.
  3. CTASmall button; the only button in the header.

  • There is no sticky state: the header scrolls away so the hero headline gets the full viewport.
  • Below 768px the links are hidden and the footer repeats them; a menu button is not needed for three links on a one-page site.

Responsive behaviour

  • < 768px: links hidden, logo + CTA only, 16px side margins.
  • ≥ 768px: links appear with --space-6 gaps.
  • The container caps at --layout-container-max (1280px) and centres.

Theming

Inherits colour from its parent: on the hero it is paper on ink, on the canvas ink on paper. The CTA variant is chosen by surface (.btn--gold on ink).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<header> landmark containing <nav aria-label="Primary">; links are links, the CTA is an <a> styled as a button because it goes to #signup.

Content

  • Three links, nouns naming sections: "How it works", "Season", "FAQ".
  • The CTA repeats the hero's verb: "Get notified".
