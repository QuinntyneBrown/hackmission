Tooltip

A short label for icon-only controls, shown on hover and on focus.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="tooltip"

Overview

Tooltips name icon-only buttons (close, dismiss) and may add a shortcut. They never hold interactive content and never carry information that is not available elsewhere.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerHas aria-label and aria-describedby.
  2. TooltipInverse surface, caption text, 300ms in / 0 out.

Variants

Text with a shortcut.

Sizes

Not applicable: One size: caption text, <code>--space-2</code> padding.

States

Visible (forced). Hidden otherwise.

Responsive behaviour

  • Flips above/below to stay in the viewport; on touch devices it shows on long-press and the aria-label covers the rest.

Theming

Inverse background and text: ink on paper in light, paper on ink in dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="tooltip" referenced by aria-describedby; shown on :hover and :focus-visible of the trigger. APG: Tooltip.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocusing the trigger shows it.
EscHides it without leaving the trigger.

Focus

Never receives focus.

Labelling

The tooltip describes; the aria-label names. They can be the same text.

Contrast

Text

Motion

Fades in over --duration-base after 300ms; instant under reduced motion.

Touch

Not relied upon on touch; the control itself is ≥ 44px.

Content

  • Two to four words: "Close video", "Dismiss".
  • Never a sentence; never the only place a fact lives.

Do and don't

Tokens

PartTokenLightDark
Surface / text--color-bg-inverse, --color-fg-inverse
Font--text-caption
Layer--z-tooltip
Motion--duration-base

Code

<button class="btn btn--ghost btn--icon has-tooltip" type="button" aria-label="Close video" aria-describedby="tip">
  <svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true">…</svg>
  <span class="tooltip" role="tooltip" id="tip">Close video <kbd>Esc</kbd></span>
</button>
Class / attributeEffect
.has-tooltipPositions the tooltip on its trigger.
.tooltipThe bubble; shown on hover/focus.
