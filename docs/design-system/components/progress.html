Progress bar

How far along a task is, or that it is running.

Overview

Progress bars show uploads and long-running jobs in the organiser tools. Use a spinner for waits under a few seconds.

Anatomy

  1. BarAccent fill.
  2. TrackSubtle background.

Variants

Determinate, indeterminate, success.

Sizes

Not applicable: One height: 8px.

States

No interactive states.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of its column.

Theming

Accent fill on subtle track; success uses the success solid.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow/min/max and a label; indeterminate omits valuenow and sets aria-busy on the container.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not interactive.

Focus

None.

Labelling

Label says what is progressing.

Contrast

Fill vs track

Motion

Indeterminate slides over --duration-deliberate; under reduced motion it pulses opacity.

Touch

—

Content

  • Pair with a label and a percentage when known.
