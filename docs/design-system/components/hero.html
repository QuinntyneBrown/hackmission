Hero

The ink band at the top of the home page: the headline, the one-field signup and the intro video.

Overview

The hero is the home page's first screen: headline with a gold emphasis, a one-line lede, the signup and the video tile, on ink. It is a composition of other components with its own tokens for ink surfaces.

Anatomy

  1. HeadlineMax 12ch, fluid 40–76px, last phrase gold.
  2. SignupInput + gold button on a row.
  3. Video tile

Responsive behaviour

  • < 1024px: one column, headline then signup then video.
  • < 640px: signup stacks; the play button moves to the tile's corner.
  • Headline is clamp(2.5rem, 6.5vw, 4.75rem); vertical padding fluid 48–112px.

Theming

All colours come from the hero tokens: --color-bg-hero, --color-fg-hero, --color-fg-hero-muted, --color-border-hero, --color-hero-danger, --color-focus-ring-on-hero. In dark mode the hero deepens to ink-950 so it still contrasts with the ink-900 surfaces.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-labelledby="hero-title"> holding the page's <h1>, the header, a form and a link.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabHeader → email → Notify me → Watch the intro.

Focus

Gold ring with an ink gap on every control.

Labelling

The h1 is the page name; the form label is visually hidden; the play link is labelled in full.

Contrast

Headline
Gold emphasis (large text)
Lede
Help text
Error text

Motion

Nothing animates; the video never autoplays.

Touch

Input and button are 44px; the play button is 44px.

Content

  • Headline: one line, a gold phrase at the end: "Code in service of the mission."
  • Lede: fragments, not sentences: "48 hours. Real problems."
  • Button: "Notify me"; help: "One email when registration opens. Unsubscribe anytime."
