Menu (dropdown)

Several actions behind one trigger, with full keyboard support.

Overview

A menu offers several actions behind one trigger. The public site has none; organiser tools will use it for per-event actions. Use a menu when a row or card has more than two actions; otherwise show the actions as buttons.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerA button with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.
  2. Itemsrole="menuitem"; icon, label, optional shortcut.
  3. Danger itemLast, after a separator, red text.

Variants

With icons, a shortcut, a section divider, a disabled item and a danger item.

Sizes

Not applicable: Menus have one size; items are 40px tall.

States

VariantDefaultHoverFocusDisabled
Item

Trigger

Closed and open trigger.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px the menu becomes a bottom sheet with the same items and 48px rows.
  • The panel flips above the trigger when there is no room below.

Theming

Panel on --color-bg-surface-raised with --shadow-3; hover rows use --color-bg-subtle; the danger item uses --color-danger-fg.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Menu button: trigger with aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded, aria-controls; panel role="menu"; items role="menuitem".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter, Space, ↓Opens and focuses the first item (↑ opens on the last).
↑ / ↓Moves between items, wrapping.
Home / EndFirst / last item.
Type a letterJumps to the next item starting with it.
EscCloses and returns focus to the trigger.
TabCloses and moves on.

Focus

Focus moves into the menu on open and back to the trigger on close.

Labelling

The panel takes aria-label or aria-labelledby the trigger. Disabled items stay focusable with aria-disabled so people learn they exist.

Contrast

Item text
Danger item
Hover row

Motion

Opens with a 200ms fade and 4px rise (--duration-base, --ease-enter); none under reduced motion.

Touch

Items are 40px tall and full width of the panel.

Content

  • Items are verb phrases: "Edit event", "Duplicate", "Delete event".
  • Group by frequency, destructive last after a separator.
  • Never more than ~7 items; split into sections or move rare actions to a page.

  <div class="menu" id="m1" role="menu" aria-label="Actions" hidden>
    <button class="menu__item" role="menuitem" type="button">Edit event</button>
    <div class="menu__divider" role="separator"></div>
    <button class="menu__item menu__item--danger" role="menuitem" type="button">Delete event</button>
  </div>
</div>
