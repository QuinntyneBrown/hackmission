Notifications

Which notification, how loud, and for how long.

Overview

Three channels: in-place results (where the action happened), toasts (brief, anywhere), banners (site-wide, persistent). Email is the fourth channel and the one the product exists for: a confirmation, then announcements.

When to use which

PriorityChannelDismiss
Confirmation of the user’s actionIn-place + polite toast8 s, pause on hover
InformationPolite toast8 s
Warning (time-limited)BannerDismissible, remembered
ErrorAssertive toast + inline alertManual only

States

  • Grouping: repeated identical toasts collapse into one with a count.
  • A notification centre is not needed on the public site.

Responsive behaviour

  • Toasts full width at the bottom on phones; banners under the header.

Accessibility

  • Polite for confirmations and info, assertive for errors; never move focus to a toast.
  • Auto-dismiss ≥ 8 s and paused on hover/focus (2.2.1).

Content

  • Email cadence promise: "Twice per event at most: when registration opens and 48 hours before it closes."
  • Toast titles are outcomes; one action verb at most.
