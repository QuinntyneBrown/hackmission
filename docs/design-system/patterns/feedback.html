Pattern

Feedback & loading

The right response for every action: inline, toast, alert or dialog; skeleton, spinner or progress.

PatternComposes toast, alert, spinner, skeleton, progress

Overview

Feedback answers "did it work?" where the person is looking. Loading answers "is it coming?" without making the layout jump.

When to use which

ActionFeedbackExample
Form submitted, stays on pageInline result + polite toastSignup success
Form failed (server)Inline alert + assertive toast with Try againSignup error
Background action completedPolite toastConfirmation email resent
Site-wide conditionBannerRegistration closing
Destructive, irreversibleDialog, then inline resultUnsubscribe
WaitIndicator
< 1 sNothing, or busy button
1–5 s, content with a known shapeSkeleton
1–5 s, no shape (dialog, page)Spinner + text
> 5 s, measurableProgress bar with value

States

  • Optimistic updates only for reversible actions, with Undo in the toast.
  • Errors persist until dismissed; success toasts auto-dismiss after 8 s and pause on hover/focus.
  • Loading states keep the final layout (skeletons match content).

Responsive behaviour

  • Toasts full width at the bottom on phones; inline results always visible where the form was.

Accessibility

  • Toast region role="status" exists before any toast; errors role="alert".
  • Focus moves to inline results after submit, never to toasts.
  • Spinners have text; skeleton containers are aria-busy.

Content

  • Lead with the outcome: "You're on the list", "Couldn't add you".
  • Loading text names the thing: "Loading video…", "Confirming your email…".
