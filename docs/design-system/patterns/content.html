Structure

What we collect

Only your email address, the date you joined and the date you confirmed. No tracking pixels in our emails.

Prose: a short heading, one or two plain sentences.

States

  • Same voice in every state: the error is as calm as the success.
  • French (Montréal) copy keeps the same length discipline; mark it lang="fr".

48 hours. Real problems.

Responsive behaviour

  • Headlines are written to fit 12ch so they hold at 360px.

Accessibility

  • Plain language; links and buttons meaningful alone; errors name the fix.

Content

  • Reuse the microcopy library; sentence case; en dashes; no exclamation marks.
