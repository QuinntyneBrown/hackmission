Iconography

Inline SVG on a 24-grid, 2px stroke, currentColor.

24-unit grid40 icons

Overview

Icons are inline SVG on a 24-unit grid with a 2px stroke, round caps and joins, drawn in currentColor. They are decorative beside text (aria-hidden) and labelled when alone. The logo mark is the one exception: a 64-unit grid with a 5-unit stroke.

Sizes

.icon--sm 16px · .icon 20px · .icon--lg 24px. Inside buttons icons are 1.25em.

Usage

  • One meaning per icon across the product.
  • Never an icon without a text label unless it is universal (close, search) and has aria-label + tooltip.
  • Status icons pair with status colours and words.

Accessibility

  • aria-hidden="true" on decorative icons.
  • Icon-only controls: aria-label.
  • Meaningful icons ≥ 3:1 against their background.

Tokens

Icons have no tokens of their own: size follows --space-4/5/6, colour follows text.
