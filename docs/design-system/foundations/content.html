Content

Overview

The voice is direct, warm and short. Headlines are statements, not slogans; body copy is fragments where a fragment will do. Sentence case everywhere. Canadian English (en-CA): organisation, colour, centre.

Voice and tone

  • Direct: "48 hours. Real problems." not "An unforgettable 48-hour experience tackling real-world problems."
  • Warm: first names in results ("Check your inbox, Amara."); a person to email when things fail.
  • Honest: "$0 to join", "Nothing. Sponsors cover it."
  • Never: "Oops", "Awesome", exclamation marks in UI, "Click here".

Casing and punctuation

  • Sentence case for headings, buttons, labels. Uppercase only for overlines and schedule dates.
  • HackMission is one word with capital H and M.
  • En dash for ranges (6–7 Feb); middle dot (·) to separate facts; curly quotes and apostrophes.

Numbers, dates, times

  • Dates: "6–7 Feb 2027"; drop the year when the section gives it.
  • Times with zone: "9:00 ET".
  • Currency: "$0" (CAD implied on a Canadian site; say CAD when amounts matter).
  • Figures as numerals: "48 hours", "4 cities".

Error message formula

What happened + why (if known) + what to do + a person for failures outside the user's control.

  • Field: "Add the end of your email address, like .ca or .com."
  • Request: "We couldn't add you just now. Our mailing service didn't respond. Your email is still in the box — try again, or write to hello@hackmission.ca."
  • Page: "This page wandered off. The link may be old — events move to a new city each season."

Empty-state formula

What is true + what will change it + one action: "The 2027 season is being planned. We're confirming host cities and partner organisations. Join the list and you'll hear the dates before anyone else. [Get notified]"

Usage

  • Reuse the library; a new verb needs a reason.
  • Keep button labels to three words.

Accessibility

  • Link and button text stands alone (2.4.4).
  • Errors identify the field and the fix (3.3.1, 3.3.3).
  • Reading level: short sentences, common words.
