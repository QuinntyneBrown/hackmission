Shape

Five radii that grow with size, and four border widths.

5 radii4 widths
  • Overview
  • Radii
  • Border widths
  • Usage
  • Accessibility
  • All shape tokens

Overview

Five radii plus full. Radius grows with size: 4px chips and code, 8px inputs and buttons, 12px cards, 16px the video tile, dialog panels and the CTA band; pills (badges, the next-date pill, the play button) are fully round. Borders are 1px, 3px for the schedule rule and 4px for alert edges.

Radii

--radius-xs 2px checkbox
--radius-sm 4px badges, inputs (compact)
--radius-md 8px buttons, inputs, cards
--radius-lg 12px dialogs, large cards
--radius-xl 16px sheets
--radius-full pills, avatars

Usage

  • Match the radius to the element's size; nested radii shrink by the padding.
  • The logo mark's cross uses rx 1.5 in a 64-unit box; do not round it further.

Accessibility

  • Focus rings follow the element's radius (outline does in modern browsers).
