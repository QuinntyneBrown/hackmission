Typography

Manrope at 500/700/800, a nine-step scale, and the role shorthands every component reads.

Overview

One family: Manrope (500 medium, 700 bold, 800 extrabold), loaded from Google Fonts in the mocks and self-hosted in production. Headlines are extrabold with tight tracking (−0.035em) and line height 1.02–1.05; body is 16px at 1.5. Numbers and step counters use the mono stack. The weight contrast in the wordmark (Hack 800 / Mission 500) runs through every page.

Measure

Prose caps at --layout-measure (65ch); marketing claims cap much shorter (headline 11–14ch, step line 18ch, pair line 22ch) so they read as statements.

Usage

  • Headings: extrabold, tight tracking, balanced wrapping (text-wrap: balance).
  • Body stays 16px; nothing below 12px (--font-size-xs) and that only for the video badge.
  • Overlines are uppercase, tracked +0.04em, bold, gold on paper.
  • Numbers that line up use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.

Accessibility

  • Sizes in rem; the page survives 200% zoom and text-spacing overrides (1.4.4, 1.4.12).
  • Line height ≥ 1.5 for body; headings may go to 1.02 because they are short.
  • Manrope has distinguishable I/l/1 at body sizes.
