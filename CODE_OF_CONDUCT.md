# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback, accountability, and a focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or imagery, trolling, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately to a project maintainer. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with the reporter's privacy and security respected.

Maintainers follow the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines][enforcement].
